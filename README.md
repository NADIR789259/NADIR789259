## 🧭 About Me

I am a **data analyst** with a Diploma in Computer Engineering and **1 year of hands-on experience in data analytics**. I clean messy datasets, find the patterns in them, and present the results as dashboards and reports that people can actually use to make decisions.

My strongest area is **Exploratory Data Analysis**, supported by SQL, Power BI and Python. I also build full applications (Django, Streamlit, FastAPI) and I am growing into **machine learning and NLP** with scikit-learn.
